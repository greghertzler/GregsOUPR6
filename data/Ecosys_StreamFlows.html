Annual Total Streamflows in Australia

Description

Australian Bureau of Meteorology, Hydrologic Reference Stations.

Format

csv file with 75 rows and 10 columns

Details

  • Water Year: time variable for years from 1951

  • Williams River: Tillegra (210011) gigalitres per water year

  • Adelaide River: Railway Bridge (G8170002) gigalitres per water year

  • Stanley River: Peachester (143303A) gigalitres per water year

  • Mosquito Creek: Struan (A2390519) gigalitres per water year

  • Snug River: Snug Tiers Road Bridge (305202) gigalitres per water year

  • Lerderderg River: Sardine Creek O'Brian Crossing (231213) gigalitres per water year

  • Yarrigil Brook: Yarrigil Formation (614044) gigalitres per water year

  • Wooroloo Brook: Karls Ranch (616001) gigalitres per water year

  • Brockman River: Yalliawirra (616019) gigalitres per water year

Annual total streamflow for water years September 1 to August 31.

Author(s)
