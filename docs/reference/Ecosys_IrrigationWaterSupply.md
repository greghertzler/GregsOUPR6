# Water Supply for Irrigated Agriculture in Australia

National data for Australia's ecosystem services: irrigated agricultural
water supply (250 m resolution): 2000-01 to 2022-23. v7. CSIRO. Data
Collection.

## Format

csv file with 23 rows and 11 columns

## Source

https://doi.org/10.25919/7jj7-8826

## Details

- Fin Year: time variable for financial year 1 July to 30 June

- Queensland: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- New South Wales: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Australian Capital Territory: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Victoria: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- South Australia: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Eastern States: sum of irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Tasmania: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Western Australia: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Northern Territory: irrigation water supplied in megalitres

- Australia: sum of irrigation water supplied in megalitres

Eastern States include Queensland, New South Wales, Australian Capital
Territory, Victoria and South Australia.
