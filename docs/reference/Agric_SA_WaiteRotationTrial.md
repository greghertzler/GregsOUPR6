# Long Term Crop Rotations, South Australia

Waite Permanent Rotation Trial. v4. CSIRO. Data Collection.

## Format

csv file with 70 rows and 30 columns

## Source

https://doi.org/10.4225/08/55E5165EC0D29

## Details

- Year: time variable in annual increments

- Plt 1-2 WF W: Wheat Fallow rotation, Wheat yields in kilograms per
  hectare

- Plt 3-4 WPe W: Wheat Peas rotation, Wheat yields in kilograms her
  hectare

- Plt 3-4 WPe Pe: Wheat Peas rotation, Pea yield in kilograms per
  hectare

- Plt 5-7 WPF W: Wheat Pasture Fallow rotation, Wheat yield in kilograms
  per hectare

- Plt 5-7 WPF P: Wheat Pasture Fallow rotation, Pasture dry matter in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 8-10 WOF W: Wheat Oats Fallow rotation, Wheat yield in kilograms
  per hectare

- Plt 8-10 WOF O: Wheat Oats Fallow rotation, Oat yield in kilograms per
  hectare

- Plt 11-16 WWPPPP W: Wheatx2 Pasturex4 rotation, Wheat yield in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 11-16 WWPPPP P: Wheatx2 Pasturex4 rotation, Pasture dry matter in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 17 W W: continuous Wheat, Wheat yield in kilograms per hectare

- Plt 18-20 WPP W: Wheat Pasturex2 rotation, Wheat yield in kilograms
  per hectare

- Plt 18-20 WPP P: Wheat Pasturex2 rotation, Pasture dry matter in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 21-23 WBPe W: Wheat Barley Peas rotation, Wheat yield in kilograms
  per hectare

- Plt 21-23 WBPe B: Wheat Barley Peas rotation, Barley yield in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 21-23 WBPe Pe: Wheat Barley Peas rotation, Pea yield in kilograms
  per hectare

- Plt 24-27 WOPF W: Wheat Oats Pasture Fallow rotation, Wheat yield in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 24-27 WOPF O: Wheat Oats Pasture Fallow rotation, Oat yield in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 24-27 WOPF P: Wheat Oats Pasture Fallow rotation, Pasture dry
  matter in kilograms per hectare

- Plt 28-29 P P: continuous Pasture, Pasture dry matter in kilograms per
  hectare

- Plt 30-33 WPPF W: Wheat Pasturex2 Fallow rotation, Wheat yield in
  kilograms per hectare

- Plt 30-33 WPPF W: Wheat Pasturex2 Fallow rotation, Pasture dry matter
  in kilograms per hectare

- Plt 34-35 WF W: Wheat Fallow rotation, Wheat yield in kilograms per
  hectare

- Annual Rain: Annual rainfall in millimetres

- Apr-Oct Rain: April through October rainfall in millimetres

- Plt 1 WF Carbon: Wheat Fallow rotation, Carbon in milligrams per
  hectare

- Plt 10 WOF Carbon: Wheat Oats Fallow rotation, Carbon in milligrams
  per hectare

- Plt 13 WWPPPP Carbon: Wheatx2 Pasturex4 rotation, Carbon in milligrams
  per hectare

- Plt 17 W Carbon: continuous Wheat, Carbon in milligrams per hectare

- Plt 29 P Carbon: continuous Pasture, Carbon in milligrams per hectare

Experimental crop yields and pasture dry matter for permanent rotations
from 1925 to 1993.
