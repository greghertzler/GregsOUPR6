# Price of Carbon

European Union Emission Trading System

## Format

csv file with 1519 rows and 6 columns

## Source

https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/EECXM

## Details

- Year: time variable as decimal year

- Open: Price at market open in EUR/tonne

- High: Daily high price in EUR/tonne

- Low: Daily low price in EUR/tonne

- Close: Price at market close in EUR/tonne

- Day: time variable in days since 1 January 2021

Daily price of Carbon Permits from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2025
