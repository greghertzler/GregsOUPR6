# Water in Farm Dams in the Riverina of New South Wales

Stock water management to improve drought resilience in intensive-use
grazing landscapes.

## Format

csv file with 19313 rows and 4 columns

## Source

corresponding author: Tim Capon <tim.capon@csiro.au>

## Details

- Run Days: time variable in daily increments

- Baseline: water volumes in cubic metres without a windbreak

- TwentyPct scenario: water volumes in cubic metres with a windbreak to
  suppress evaporation by 20%

- FortyPct scenario: water volumes in cubic metres with a windbreak to
  suppress evaporation by 40%

MatLab simulated water volumes for a 4,000 cubic metre dam.
