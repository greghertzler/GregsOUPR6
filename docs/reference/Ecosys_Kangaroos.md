# Kangaroo Population and Harvest in South Australia

Population surveys and meat processor records of harvest.

## Format

csv file with 46 rows and 7 columns

## Source

https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/topics/animals-and-plants/sustainable-use-of-animals-and-plants/kangaroo-conservation-and-management/quotas-harvest-data

## Details

- Year: time variable

- Red Pop: Estimated population of Red Kangaroos in 1000 head

- Red Harvest: Number of Red Kangaroos commercially harvested in 1000
  head

- Grey Pop: Estimated population of Western Grey Kangaroos in 1000 head

- Grey Harvest: Number of Western Grey Kangaroos commercially harvested
  in 1000 head

- Euro Pop: Estimated population of European Kangaroos in 1000 head

- Euro Harvest: Number of European Kangaroos commercially harvested in
  1000 head

Regions are aggregated for all of South Australia.

## Author

Government of South Australia, Department of Environment
